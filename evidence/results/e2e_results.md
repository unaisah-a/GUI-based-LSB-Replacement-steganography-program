## What AUTHENTIC means here

AUTHENTIC means the signed verification record and the recovered message match, and the signature verified against the supplied public key. It does not mean every part of the cover media is unchanged, and it does not by itself reject a replayed file.

## Why several failures share one verdict

An extraction failure does not identify its cause. A wrong LSB depth, a wrong start location or secret, an absent payload and sample corruption produce indistinguishable bit streams.
